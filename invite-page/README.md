# Getting started with ZMR Rent

You got this because your manager, or ZMR's upper management, added you to the **ZMR Rent** app.
The app is how ZMR Mobility records renters, vehicles and rent payments. This page explains the
link you were sent and how to install the app. It takes about five minutes.

## What you need

- An **Android phone** (Android 8 or newer). The app is not available for iPhone.
- The **WhatsApp message or email** from your manager with your invite link.
- About **5 MB** of free space and an internet connection for the download.

## What the link is

The message has one link. On your phone, it opens a ZMR Rent page with two steps:

1. **Download ZMR Rent**: gets the app file (`ZMR-Rent.apk`).
2. **Open my invite in ZMR Rent**: sets up your account with the details your manager entered
   (your name, employee ID, mobile, city and who you report to).

The invite is made for you only. **Do not forward it**: anyone with it can set up your account.

## Install the app

1. Open the link on your Android phone and tap **Download ZMR Rent**.
2. If you are asked, choose to download the file. When it finishes, tap **Open**.
3. ZMR Rent comes straight from ZMR, not from the Play Store, so the phone asks once for
   permission:
   - If you see *"your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*, tap
     **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, and go back.
   - If **Play Protect** shows a warning, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.
4. Tap **Install**, then **Done**.

## Set up your account

1. Go back to the invite page, or tap the link in the message again.
2. Tap **Open my invite in ZMR Rent**. The app opens with your details filled in.
3. Check that the name and employee ID are yours.
4. Choose a **4-digit PIN** and type it twice. You will use it every time you open the app.
   Do not share your PIN.
5. Tap **Set up this phone**. You are now logged in.

**If the button does not open the app:** on the invite page, tap **Copy invite code**. Open
ZMR Rent, tap **Log in**, then **I have an invite code**, paste the code, and carry on from step 3.

## Using the app

The main screen has five buttons:

| Button | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Register customer** | Add a new renter or lessee, their vehicle, documents and first payment |
| **Renters** | Current renters, with any past-due amount beside each name |
| **Inventory** | All vehicles, by city |
| **Send data** | Send your Excel report to your manager by WhatsApp or email |
| **Other (⋯)** | Language (English, हिन्दी, தமிழ், ಕನ್ನಡ), My email, and manager tools |

Everything you enter is kept on your phone, and it works without signal. Your manager sees your
work only when you tap **Send data**, so send it whenever your manager asks.

## Common questions

**I forgot my PIN.** Ask your manager to send you a new invite. Open it on the same phone and
choose a new PIN. Your records stay.

**I got a new phone.** Ask your manager for a new invite and set up the new phone with it. Your
old phone's records stay on the old phone, so send your report from it first.

**I got an update link.** Download and install it the same way. Your records and login stay;
there is no need to set up again.

**The link opens a page that says "no invite".** The link was cut short when it was copied.
Ask your manager to send it again.

**Who do I ask for help?** Your manager. Their name and number are shown on the setup screen.
