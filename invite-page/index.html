You have been invited to the ZMR Rent app. Do these two steps on your Android phone.
आपको ZMR Rent ऐप में जोड़ा गया है। अपने Android फ़ोन पर ये दो काम करें।

1Download and install the app

ऐप डाउनलोड करके इंस्टॉल करें

Download ZMR Rent

Ask your manager for the download link.

  1. Open the downloaded file (ZMR-Rent.apk).
  2. If the phone says "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps", tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect asks, tap More details → Install anyway.

ZMR Rent को Play Store से नहीं, सीधे ZMR से इंस्टॉल किया जाता है, इसलिए फ़ोन एक बार अनुमति माँगता है।

2Set up your account

अपना अकाउंट सेट अप करें

Come back to this page after installing, then tap:

Open my invite in ZMR Rent

The app opens with your details filled in. Check they are yours, choose a 4-digit PIN, and tap Set up this phone.

ऐप आपकी जानकारी के साथ खुलेगा। जाँचें, 4 अंकों का PIN चुनें और "Set up this phone" दबाएँ।

If the button does not open the app

  1. Tap Copy invite code below.
  2. Open ZMR Rent, tap Log in, then I have an invite code.
  3. Paste the code, choose your PIN and tap Set up this phone.

Keep this invite to yourself: anyone with it can set up your account.
यह इनवाइट किसी और को न भेजें।
